### Sphinx
#### Duration
3 minutes
#### Description
Lie down on your belly. Move the elbows just ahead of your shoulders, propping yourself up.
#### Sensation
Lower back. If the sensations are too strong, move your elbows further ahead, lowering your chest closer to the floor. Secondary target is front torso.
#### CounterPose
Relax on abdomen, turn your head to the right, and draw right knee up beside you on the floor (1 minute).
#### Meridians
- Urinary Bladder
- Kidney
- Stomach
- Spleen

### Seal
#### Duration
2 minutes
#### Description
Turn your hands outward, straighten the arms (locking the elbows) and walk the hands towards you.
#### Sensation
You may feel the greatest amount of compression in the low back if your hands are not right under your shoulders but slightly forward. Secondary target is front torso.
#### CounterPose
Relax on abdomen, turn your head to the left, and draw left knee up beside you on the floor (1 minute).
#### Meridians
- Urinary Bladder
- Kidney
- Stomach
- Spleen
