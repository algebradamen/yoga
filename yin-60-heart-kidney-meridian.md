## Yin Yoga – 60 minutes
**Theme:** Grounding the Heart · Opening Breath · Emotional Balance  
**Primary Meridians:** Heart, Lung  
**Secondary Support:** Kidney, Urinary Bladder

---

## Opening Meditation – 3 minutes

**Shape:** Comfortable seat or constructive rest  

**Description:**  
Arrive in stillness. Feel the weight of the body supported by the ground.  
Invite awareness to the natural rhythm of the breath — without changing it.  
Set an intention connected to the heart: openness, compassion, or presence.

---

## Dangling – 3 minutes  
*(Standing Forward Fold – Yin version)*

**Meridians:**  
• Urinary Bladder, Kidney (foundation)

**Purpose:**  
Ground the nervous system before opening the chest and breath.

### Rebound – 1–2 minutes  
Constructive rest or standing pause.

---

## Supported Squat (Malasana) – 3 minutes  

**Meridians:**  
• Kidney, Liver, Spleen  

**Energetic role:**  
Creates stability and rooting — preparing for heart opening later.

### Rebound – 1 minute  
Seated stillness.

---

## Dragonfly (Seated Straddle)

### Fold over right leg – 3 minutes  
**Counterpose:** Windshield wipers – 1 minute

### Fold over left leg – 3 minutes  
**Counterpose:** Windshield wipers – 1 minute

### Fold down the middle – 3 minutes  
**Counterpose:** Windshield wipers – 1 minute

**Meridians:**  
• Liver, Kidney, Urinary Bladder  

**Note:**  
Keep the spine relaxed — no pulling. Allow breath to widen the back ribs.

---

## Sphinx – 3 minutes  

**Meridians:**  
• Urinary Bladder, Kidney  
• Secondary: **Lung & Heart pathway through chest**

**Awareness cue:**  
Notice the breath moving into the upper ribs and heart space.

### Counterpose – 1 minute  
Rest on belly, head turned right, right knee drawn up.

---

## 💗 Supported Fish – 4 minutes  
*(NEW – Heart & Lung focus)*

**Description:**  
Lie back over a bolster placed horizontally under the upper back and heart.  
Head and neck supported. Legs extended or bent, arms opening wide with palms up.

**Meridians & Energetic Focus:**  
• **Heart & Lung (primary)**  

**Sensation & Awareness:**  
• Expansion across chest, collarbones, shoulders  
• Breath naturally deepening  
• Emotional openness or vulnerability may arise  

**Yin Reminder:**  
Support is essential — this pose should feel *held*, not strained.

### Rebound – 1 minute  
Shavasana or constructive rest.

---

## Swan (Yin Pigeon)

### Right Side  
**Full Swan:** 1 minute  
**Sleeping Swan:** 3 minutes  

**Counterpose:** Child’s Pose – 1 minute

### Left Side  
**Full Swan:** 1 minute  
**Sleeping Swan:** 3 minutes  

### Rebound – 1 minute  
Shavasana

**Meridians:**  
• Liver, Gallbladder  
*(creates emotional release before heart work)*

---

## 💨 Melting Heart (Anahatasana) – 4 minutes  
*(NEW – Lung emphasis)*

**Description:**  
From tabletop, keep hips over knees and slowly melt the chest toward the floor.  
Forehead or chin rests on support. Arms extended or bent.

**Meridians & Energetic Focus:**  
• **Lung (primary)**  
• Heart (secondary)

**Sensation:**  
• Chest, shoulders, armpits  
• Breath awareness — especially inhalation  

### Rebound – 1 minute  
Child’s Pose.

---

## Reclined Butterfly (Supta Baddha Konasana) – 5 minutes  

**Description:**  
Lie back over a bolster lengthwise. Soles of feet together, knees supported.  
Arms may open wide or rest on the belly and heart.

**Meridians:**  
• **Heart & Lung (strong)**  

**Cue:**  
Feel the breath gently lifting the heart.

### Rebound – 1 minute  
Shavasana.

---

## Reclined Twist – 3 minutes each side  

**Meridians:**  
• Gallbladder, Urinary Bladder  
• **Secondary: Heart via gentle spinal rotation**

### Rebound – 1 minute each side  
Shavasana.

---

## Final Shavasana – 8–10 minutes  

**Description:**  
Total rest.  
Invite softness behind the heart and an easy, effortless breath.  
Allow the effects of the practice to integrate fully.

---

**Total time:** ~60 minutes
